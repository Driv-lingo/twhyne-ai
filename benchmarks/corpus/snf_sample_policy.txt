Sunrise Skilled Nursing Facility — Sample Internal Policy Excerpt (fictional, for benchmarking only)

Section 4.2 — Fall Risk Assessment.
Every resident must receive a fall risk assessment within 8 hours of admission
and again after any fall. The facility uses the Morse Fall Scale. A resident
with a Morse score of 45 or higher is classified as high fall risk and must
have a yellow wristband and a bed alarm activated.

Section 4.3 — Post-Fall Protocol.
After any resident fall, staff must: (1) not move the resident until a nurse
assesses for injury, (2) take vital signs within 15 minutes, (3) notify the
attending physician and the resident's designated contact within 1 hour, and
(4) complete an incident report before the end of the shift.

Section 6.1 — Medication Pass Timing.
Scheduled medications may be administered within a 60-minute window before or
after the ordered time. Insulin ordered before meals must be given within 30
minutes of the meal, not the standard 60-minute window.

Section 9.4 — PHI Handling.
Resident protected health information must never be transmitted to any external
cloud service. All documentation stays on facility-managed systems. Portable
devices storing PHI must be encrypted at rest.
